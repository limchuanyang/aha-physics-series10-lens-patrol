HOW TO PLAY

Cards fall from the top. Sort each one into the NEGATIVE (−) gate on the left or the POSITIVE (+) gate on the right.

Keys: ←/A = NEGATIVE, →/D = POSITIVE (sorts the lowest card). ESC = pause.
Touch/Mouse: drag a card into a gate, or use the − / + buttons.

Wrong sort or missed card = lose a life. Chain answers for COMBOS. Sort fast for the CRITICAL INSIGHT bonus.
